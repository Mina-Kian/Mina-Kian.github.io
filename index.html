Mina Kian

I am a PhD candidate in the Interaction Lab at the University of Southern California, advised by Prof. Maja Matarić.

My research focuses on evaluating socially assistive robots (SARs) in mental health contexts, primarily for cognitive behavioral therapy homework support. I am also interested in foundation model assessments, evaluating them in vision-based affective tasks and their ability to support mental health dialogue.

Outside of research, I am an avid skier. Ski photo

Research

I study how socially assistive robots and foundation models can support mental health interventions. My work examines cognitive behavioral therapy homework support, affective reasoning, and human-robot interaction in emotionally sensitive settings.
