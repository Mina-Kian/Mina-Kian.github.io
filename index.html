Overview

I am a PhD candidate in the Interaction Lab at the University of Southern California, advised by Prof. Maja Matarić. My research focuses on evaluating socially assistive robots (SARs) in mental health contexts, primarily for cognitive behavioral therapy homework support. I am also interested in foundation model assessments, evaluating them in vision-based affective tasks and their ability to support mental health dialogue.
